About SkillSync

SkillSync is a student skill-exchange platform where you learn, teach and connect with peers based on skills and interests.

  • 🪙 Earn Skill Tokens by teaching, spend them to learn
  • 👤 Build a skill profile and showcase achievements
  • 👥 Find teammates for projects
  • 📄 Generate a resume from your profile
  • 💬 Get help from the built-in assistant

Version: v1.4.2
Built for: Advanced Web Technologies (AWT) project

Terms & Conditions

Last updated: October 2026

1. Acceptance

By creating an account you agree to use SkillSync responsibly and follow these terms.

2. Your account

You are responsible for your login details and all activity on your account. Provide accurate information and keep your password private.

3. Skill Tokens

Skill Tokens have no cash value. They can only be earned and spent inside SkillSync and cannot be sold or transferred outside the platform.

4. Acceptable use

Do not harass others, share false information, spam, or misuse the platform. Teaching sessions must be honest and respectful.

5. Content

You own the content you add to your profile and projects, and allow SkillSync to display it according to your privacy settings.

6. Termination

We may suspend accounts that break these terms. You can delete your account at any time from Settings.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Data we collect

Name, email, password (stored encrypted), profile details, skills, projects, wallet activity, and sign-in details (IP address and device).

How we use it

To run your account, match you with peers, show your profile as per your privacy settings, secure your account and improve SkillSync.

Your controls

Choose who can see your profile, hide your email and phone number, manage notifications and review your login activity in Settings.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Information is shown to other users only as allowed by your settings.

Deleting your data

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, wallet, resume, settings and support messages.
